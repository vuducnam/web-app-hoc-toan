## Skills-to-Generator Mapping

### Proceduralizable `[gen]` — Seeded by Algorithm

- All whole-number arithmetic (addition, subtraction, multiplication, division)
- Place-value operations, rounding, expanded form
- Decimal operations (add, sub, mult, div)
- Fraction operations (simplify, common denominator, add, sub, mult, div)
- Unit conversions (length, mass, capacity, area)
- Volume calculations (rectangular box, simple prisms)
- Time-based calculation (elapsed time, speed × time)

### Hand-Authored Pool `[pool]` — Require Content Bank

- Word problems with context (shopping, measurement, scenarios)
- Geometry construction & proofs (parallel lines, angle properties)
- Data interpretation (tables, graphs, statistics)
- Shapes & area (triangles, trapezoids, circles) — requires diagram UI
- Ratio & percentage applied problems
- Probability intuition tasks
